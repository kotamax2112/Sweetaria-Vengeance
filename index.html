    Lore of Sweetaria

    TEEN TROLL
    The Cell Phone Teen Troll

    Born from the digital waste of a thousand unread notifications, she haunts the mid-levels of Sweetaria. She does not fight with swords, but with pure, weaponized apathy.

    Legend says she was once a regular girl who stared into the abyss of a scrolling feed until the feed stared back. Now, she guards the Gateway to the Upper Stratum, ensuring that only those with "no chill" may pass. Her thumbs move faster than the speed of light, and her primary attack is 'The Ratio'—a devastating beam of negative engagement.

    BIG BOSS HEAD
    The Big Boss Head

    A manifestation of pure corporate generational spite. The Big Boss Head floats eternally at the summit of the spire, fueled by the tears of millennials and the fluctuating housing market.

    He possesses no body, for he needs no hands to crush dreams—only policy changes. He believes the world stopped turning in 1985 and that Sweetaria's resources are best kept in an offshore cloud account. Defeating him requires not just strength, but the ultimate weapon: Kindness (and perhaps a little bit of systemic reform).

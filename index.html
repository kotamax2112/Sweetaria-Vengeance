LORE

The Cell Phone Teen Troll
Born from the digital waste of a thousand unread notifications, she haunts the mid-levels of Sweetaria. She fights not with swords, but with pure, weaponized apathy. Legend says she stared into the abyss of a scrolling feed until the feed stared back. Her primary attack is 'The Ratio'—a devastating beam of negative engagement.
The Big Boss Head
A manifestation of pure corporate spite. The Big Boss Head floats eternally at the summit of the spire, fueled by the tears of millennials. He possesses no body, for he needs no hands to crush dreams—only policy changes. He believes the world stopped turning in 1985 and that his only weakness is Kindness (which he finds "disgusting").
